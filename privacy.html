Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy covers the Opteva client onboarding form. Opteva ("we," "us," or "our") uses this form to set up your account and content system. This policy explains what we collect here, how we use it, and your choices. Our main site's Privacy Policy covers opteva.ai and the Content Studio app; this page covers onboarding specifically.

Information We Collect

To set up your account, this form collects:

  • Contact details: your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
  • Business details: your business name, website, target audience, and the service you want us to lead with.
  • Brand assets: a logo file you upload, and the brand colors you choose.
  • Your social and booking links: any Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, X/Twitter, Pinterest, or Google Business Profile handles you provide, your booking link, and any other links you add.
  • Your goals: what you're looking to achieve, your current posting frequency, and anything else you tell us in the notes field.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create your client account and configure your brand profile inside the Opteva Content Studio.
  • To generate content that matches your brand, in your brand's colors and voice.
  • To contact you about your account and onboarding.

Where This Information Is Stored

Submissions from this form, including your uploaded logo, are stored in a private Google Sheet and Google Drive folder accessible only to the Opteva team, and are then used to set up your account in the Opteva Content Studio (governed by our main Privacy Policy).

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run onboarding and hosting (such as Google), or if required by law.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep your onboarding information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This form is for business owners and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with an updated date.
